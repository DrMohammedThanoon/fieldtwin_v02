## Classification of candidate A (27.729° N, 40.392° E)
**Under-recognized candidate / new proposed mission use.**
- Not "first-ever": dune and desert analogs are common worldwide, and a fuller search (Arabic sources, Saudi Space Agency, KAUST) was **not** done.
- What is new here: (1) selection by *measured* terrain geometry matched to Jezero at a common 60 m scale; (2) a navigation test across a sand-to-bedrock transition; (3) in a Saudi area with no documented rover-analog use found.

Lake Salda remains the stronger **geological** Jezero analog. FieldTwin does not compete with it on geology.
